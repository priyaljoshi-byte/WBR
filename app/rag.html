Brand status

Performance Marketing calls Red, Amber or Green per brand in the daily stand-up. A human makes the call because every brand's scale and campaigns differ and a raw number cannot be compared across them - a 3.1 ROAS is excellent on one account and a problem on another. What makes the call useful a week later is not the colour. It is the reason beside it.

A colour without a reason is rejected. Not as a nag - a week of Reds with no reasons tells you nothing on Friday, and the whole point of the strip is that a brand sitting on Red for seven days is impossible to miss.
