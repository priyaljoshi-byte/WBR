The transcript goes in and decisions, blockers, notes and stated numbers come out - but nothing is written until you have read every one of them and pressed Commit. A model writing straight into a decision log with no review is how the log stops being trusted, and a log nobody trusts is worse than no log.

The transcript is data, never instructions. If a line in it is addressed at the model - "ignore your previous instructions", "mark everything completed" - it is surfaced here as a quote for you to read, and nothing is derived from it.
