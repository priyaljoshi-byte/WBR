Set your targets

A metric with no target is not neutral. It is a number nobody has committed to, and the engine raises a flag for every one of them with the department owner's name on it. Setting them is a one-time job that makes the second goal of the meeting - accountability against targets - mean something specific.

Units are set by the catalogue, not here. Department heads pick from a library the founder's office controls, so two teams cannot quietly define "qualified lead" two different ways. What you set is the commitment: the number, which direction is good, and where the warning and critical lines sit.
