Hiring funnel - role by role

Read live from the hiring tracker each time this page opens. The ring is pipeline health: live candidates at Round 1 or later against what each opening needs. Click a role to see its waterfall - how many reached each stage, and where people stopped.

Names, roles and stages only. The tracker also holds phone numbers, emails and pay; this page never shows them. Stage words and role spellings are matched in config/hiring.json - anything it does not recognise is listed at the bottom, not hidden.
